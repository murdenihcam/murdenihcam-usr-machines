Custom USR machines for the Elektron Machinedrum on the MDX X.20 firmware, focused on sample mangling and effects processing.

Let me know how you get on with these and if you have any suggestions!

Discussion: Custom USR Machines for X.20 on Elektronauts, and the mdx-usr-machines thread on the MDX Discord.

How to install

  1. Update your MD to MDX X.20. Back up first; third-party machines are installed at your own risk.
  2. On the MD: FUNC+GLOBAL → FILE → MACHINE MGR, pick an empty slot, choose RECV (it shows WAITING…).
  3. Send the .syx with SysEx Librarian or your SysEx app of choice. Several files can go at once; they fill empty slots.
  4. The new machines show up under KIT → EDIT.

Versions that read all banks or the UW ROM bank need a Machinedrum UW. E12 bank versions and the NFX effect work on all models.

Disclaimer: Back up your Machinedrum (sounds, kits, patterns) before installing anything, firmware updates included. These machines are tested and run smoothly on a UW+ MKII, but you download and use them at your own risk; they come as they are, with no guarantee.
